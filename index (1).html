N1 — PROFILE

Field-tested, then formalized

Kehinde's engineering started years before the degree did — repairing gadgets and running surface wiring as a teenage apprentice, long before multivariable control theory entered the picture at Obafemi Awolowo University. That order shows: his final-year project applied Internal Model Control strategy to multivariable systems, while his day-to-day work has meant conduit wiring at housing estates, CCTV rigs at filling stations, and inverter installs in client homes. He now runs YusRiKTek, his own electrical and telecommunication service company, managing installs end-to-end — quoting, scheduling, wiring, and client handover.
